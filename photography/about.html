About David

A headshot shouldn't just show what you look like; it should hint at what you are capable of playing. Having been on both sides of the casting process, I know exactly what casting directors are looking for when they open a thumbnail.

The Photographer

I have spent years working with actors in Copenhagen and across Europe. My goal behind the lens is to create a relaxed, rigorous space where you can drop your 'photo face' and allow your actual, specific humanity to be captured.

Also a Coach

Because I also work as an acting coach, my photography sessions feel more like scene study. We aren't just taking photos; we are finding characters, intentions, and thoughts.
